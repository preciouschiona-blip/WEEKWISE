# WeekWise privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

WeekWise is a weekly planner for Android. This page explains what the app does with your information.

## In short

Everything you enter stays on your phone. WeekWise has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. The app does not connect to the internet to send your data anywhere.

## What the app stores

Your tasks, classes, routine times and settings. They are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Permissions and why the app asks for them

- **Notifications:** to show your reminders and the nightly "plan tomorrow" prompt.
- **Alarms and wake lock:** to ring your wake-up and routine alarms on time, even when the app is closed, and to start them again after your phone restarts.
- **Calendar (read only, optional):** to show events from your phone's calendar in your week. The app never changes or deletes calendar events.
- **Contacts (through the system contact picker):** when you tap "Pick from contacts", Android shows its own picker and gives the app only the one name and number you choose. The app has no access to the rest of your contacts.
- **Phone calls:** tapping Call opens your phone's dialer with the number filled in. You press the call button yourself.

## Sharing

WeekWise does not sell, share or send your information to anyone. Links you add (for example a Google Meet link) open in your browser or the matching app.

## Children

WeekWise is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text appears on this page. Questions can be sent through the issues page of this repository: https://github.com/preciouschiona-blip/WEEKWISE/issues
